## 📖 Complete Courses & Books

### Free Online Courses
- [fast.ai Practical Deep Learning](https://course.fast.ai/) — top-down, practical approach
- [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn) — short, hands-on mini-courses
- [Google ML Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course) — free
- [Stanford CS229 (ML)](https://cs229.stanford.edu/) — rigorous mathematical foundation
- [Stanford CS231n (Computer Vision)](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/)

### Books (Free or Open Source)
- [Python Data Science Handbook](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/) — Jake VanderPlas
- [Hands-On ML with Scikit-Learn & TensorFlow](https://github.com/ageron/handson-ml3) — Aurélien Géron
- [Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville
- [Pattern Recognition & Machine Learning](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/uploads/prod/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf) — Bishop

### Practice Platforms
- [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/) — competitions + free GPU
- [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/) — coding practice
- [Project Euler](https://projecteuler.net/) — mathematical programming
- [HackerRank Python](https://www.hackerrank.com/domains/python) — graded exercises
